💬 AI Study Chat ×
What's the difference between the light reactions and the Calvin cycle?
Great question! Based on your notes:

Light reactions happen in the thylakoid membranes and use light to produce ATP and NADPH.

Calvin cycle happens in the stroma and uses those ATP/NADPH to fix CO₂ into glucose 🌿
And what does chlorophyll do exactly?
Chlorophyll is the pigment that absorbs light — mainly red and blue wavelengths. It's what makes plants green because green light is reflected, not absorbed.
