# NotesOS — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users

**Who they are:** Students — primarily undergraduates and self-directed learners — using NotesOS during active study sessions, exam prep, and collaborative review.

**Their context:** They're often multitasking across a lecture, a PDF, and their notes simultaneously. They come to this app when they want to *actually understand* something, not just collect content. The interface competes with distraction.

**The job to be done:** Transform raw uploaded material (PDFs, voice notes, slides) into something they can learn from — quizzes, AI-synthesized summaries, knowledge maps — and track genuine mastery over time. Studying should feel satisfying, not clinical.

**Emotional goal:** Users should feel like they're using something that *cares* about them learning — personal, warm, a little delightful. Not a corporate SaaS tool. More like a favourite notebook come to life.

---

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** Creative, playful, bright

**Voice:** Encouraging without being patronising. Celebrates progress. Speaks like a brilliant, slightly irreverent study partner — not a professor. Short sentences. Light in tone.

**Tone in UI copy:** Active, specific, warm. "Let's see what you know" beats "Start quiz". "Nice work" beats "Quiz complete".

**What this is NOT:**
- Not edtech corporate (Coursera, Canvas — sterile, bureaucratic)
- Not children's app (too cute, too saturated)
- Not productivity minimalism (Notion, Linear — too cold and adult)
- Not grunge/distressed or scrapbook chaos

---

### Aesthetic Direction

**Core metaphor:** A creative student's physical notebook — one that has been used, loved, annotated, doodled in. The interface has texture and humanity. Every surface feels like it has been *touched*.

**Visual theme:** Half notebook, half polished app. The notebook character lives in specific surfaces and accents — not everywhere at once. Content and typography still lead; the craft is the supporting character.

**Background:**
- Base: `#F5F3EE` warm cream (slightly warmer than current `#f0eeea`)
- Overlay: SVG noise/grain texture at 4–6% opacity simulating paper grain
- Never a flat colour — the page has subtle life

**Accent — Highlighter amber:**
- Not flat amber. A *highlighter mark*: `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)` with slight blur
- Used for: active sidebar topic, audio player fill, selected states, quiz correct answers, key section headers
- Key Points section header gets a hand-drawn SVG underline in amber

**Illustrations — Hand-drawn ink style:**
- Rough, ink-line SVG illustrations as decorative accents
- Style: fine-line technical illustration meets notebook doodle — stippled dots, single-weight strokes, slightly imperfect lines
- Subject matter: study-related (lightbulb, headphones, pencil, open book, brain, stars) — drawn loosely, not icon-polished
- Placed in: section headers, empty states, focus mode margins, onboarding, loading states
- Reference aesthetic: dotted stipple illustration style (see Paper app screenshot)

**Typography additions:**
- Add **Caveat** (Google Fonts) as a third handwriting accent font
- Use ONLY for: small UI labels, section annotations, metadata, breadcrumbs, audio subtitle ("Generated from N sources"), margin annotations
- Never for body text, headings, or anything needing authority
- Feels like a student wrote a small label in the margin

**Notebook ruled lines:**
- Consolidated note section gets very subtle horizontal ruled lines behind text
- 28px apart, 6% opacity, colour `#8B7355`
- Like reading notes on real notebook paper

**Rough card borders:**
- The consolidated note card and key points card use a slightly organic border (SVG filter or rough.js-style path)
- All other cards keep clean borders — only the main study surfaces get the notebook treatment

**Focus mode margins:**
- At viewport widths > 1100px, small static SVG doodles appear in the left and right margins of the content column
- Not interactive — purely decorative, like margin doodles from a student who listens while drawing

**Empty states:**
- Every empty state gets a hand-drawn illustration (single colour: warm grey or amber)
- "No notes yet" → open notebook with blank pages
- "No quiz history" → pencil and question mark doodle
- "Generating audio…" → headphones with hand-sketched sound wave arcs

---

### Dark Mode — Night Journal

Dark mode is supported now, using a "night journal" direction:
- Deep warm darks (not cool blue-black) — base: around `#1C1A17`, surface: `#252320`
- Same grain texture overlaid on dark surfaces
- Amber highlight glows slightly more vivid in dark context
- Caveat labels and notebook lines carry across with adjusted opacity

---

### Accessibility Baseline

**Standard:** WCAG AA throughout (4.5:1 contrast minimum for text)

**Implementation notes:**
- Grain/texture must never impede text legibility — always checked at minimum contrast
- Rough SVG borders must not reduce perceived hit targets or interactive affordances
- Illustrations are decorative (`aria-hidden="true"`) — never carry meaning
- Caveat font only used for non-critical labels; never for required information only
- Animation: respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — skip grain shimmer, fade doodles in without translation

---

### Design Principles

1. **Texture before flatness.** Every surface should feel like it has weight and grain. Flat colour is the last resort, not the default.

2. **Playful restraint.** The hand-drawn accents, Caveat labels, and illustrations are supporting characters — they amplify content without competing with it.

3. **Study-forward.** Design decisions should make studying feel satisfying and even a little exciting. Delight is not optional; it is the retention mechanism.

4. **Notebook logic.** If something would exist in a well-loved student's physical notebook — a highlighter mark, a ruled line, a margin doodle — it belongs here. If it wouldn't, question it.

5. **Both modes, same soul.** Light mode is warm paper. Dark mode is a night journal. Different palettes, same personality — the amber accent and grain texture are constants.

### What To Do When Designing

- **New empty state?** Add a hand-drawn illustration. Single colour. Sketchy.
- **Active/selected state?** Use the highlighter amber, not a filled background.
- **Small label or metadata?** Consider Caveat font — but only if it won't carry critical information.
- **Main study surface card?** Rough border treatment. Notebook lines behind text if it's a notes section.
- **Loading state?** Illustrated. Headphones, pencil, sparkle — drawn loosely.
- **Wide viewport (>1100px)?** Margin doodles in focus mode — static, not distracting.
- **Dark mode?** Warm darks. Same amber. Same grain.
