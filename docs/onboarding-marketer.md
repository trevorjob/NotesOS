## The One-Liner

**NotesOS turns your course materials into a complete study system — automatically.**

---

## The Problem

Students waste huge amounts of time doing the wrong kind of studying.

They re-read their notes passively. They highlight without testing themselves. They spend more time organising than actually learning. And the tools they use — Notion, Google Docs, even Anki — make them build their own study system from scratch every single semester.

The AI tools that exist (ChatGPT, Perplexity) are general-purpose. They don't know your course material. They don't track what you've learned. They don't generate quizzes based on your actual notes.

Students are overwhelmed. The content pile is huge. Exam season hits and they still feel unready. There's no tool that takes your raw material and turns it into something you can actually learn from — until now.

---

## What NotesOS Does

You upload your course materials — PDFs, lecture slides, handwritten notes, anything. NotesOS does the rest.

**It automatically generates:**

- A clean, readable summary of your material (like a study guide written just for you)
- A bullet-point list of the key takeaways
- A glossary of core concepts and definitions
- An audio lesson you can listen to on the go
- Practice quizzes — multiple choice and short answer, graded by AI

**You can also:**

- Chat with an AI tutor that has actually read your specific materials
- Record your quiz answers by voice (the AI transcribes and grades them)
- Track your mastery level per topic
- See your study streak, time studied, and average quiz score
- Share your semester with classmates so you're all studying from the same pool
- Create custom practice tests — choose which topics, how many questions, what difficulty

**And there's a Focus Mode** — a full-screen distraction-free reading view that strips away everything except the content. Small doodles appear in the margins on wide screens, like a notebook you'd actually love to study in.

## Who It's For

**Primary:** University and college students — undergrads especially.

**Profile:** Overwhelmed with content. Using a bunch of disconnected tools. Motivated to do well but struggling to study *efficiently* rather than just *a lot*. Already using ChatGPT but finding it doesn't really know their course material. Comfortable with tech.

**Sweet spot:** Students preparing for exams, reviewing lectures, studying with classmates in the same course.

**Secondary:** Self-directed learners — people upskilling, studying for certifications, working through a structured reading list.

## What It Feels Like

The aesthetic is deliberately unlike anything else in edtech. It feels like a creative student's physical notebook come to life — warm, textured, a little handmade. There are hand-drawn doodles in the margins of focus mode, a paper-like grain texture on every surface, a hand-written style font for small annotations.

The brand voice is the same: encouraging without being patronising. "Let's see what you know" not "Start quiz". Like a brilliant, slightly irreverent study partner — not a professor, not a corporate tool.

**Three words: creative, playful, bright.**

## The Vision

**NotesOS is your study partner — one with a PhD in how humans learn.**

Most study tools help you organise information. NotesOS is building something different: an AI study partner that actually understands how memory works, how retention happens, and how understanding is built — and applies that knowledge to your specific material, in the way that works for your brain.

The long-term vision is a platform rooted in cognitive psychology and learning science. Not just "here's a quiz" — but the right quiz, at the right moment, in the right format, because that's what research says actually makes information stick. Spaced repetition, active recall, retrieval practice, elaborative interrogation — all of it applied automatically, invisibly, so you just feel like you're learning better.

**NotesOS follows you through your entire university journey.** From your first year to your last. It knows every topic you've ever studied, every quiz you've taken, every concept you struggled with. It gets better at teaching you the longer you use it — and it doesn't forget.

**It gets smarter the more students join.** When your classmates are on NotesOS too, everyone benefits. Shared knowledge pools, stronger note coverage, collective understanding — the platform compounds. One student's insights become everyone's. A course that 50 people are studying together becomes richer than one any individual could build alone.

**The goal is to become the first thing students reach for** — before a test, before an exam, when they need to remember something, when they want to actually understand a topic and not just pass it. Not a tool you use occasionally. The central almanac of your academic life.

**Where we're going next:**

- Better onboarding so new users immediately see the magic
- Mobile app
- Smarter progress analytics — "these topics are at risk before your exam"
- AI-generated study schedules based on mastery gaps and upcoming dates
- Learning science features: spaced repetition scheduling, active recall prompts, confidence tracking

**Longer term:**

- The platform gets more powerful as more students from the same course or institution join
- Deep personalisation — NotesOS adapts to how *you specifically* learn best over time
- Becomes the canonical study resource for entire cohorts, not just individuals
