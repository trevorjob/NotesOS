# NotesOS

---

## What is NotesOS?

NotesOS is an AI study partner — one with the knowledge of someone with a PhD in how humans learn and retain information.

You upload your course materials — PDFs, lecture slides, handwritten notes, anything — and NotesOS transforms them into a complete learning system: a clean summary, key points, core concepts, an audio lesson, and AI-generated quizzes. There's also an AI tutor you can chat with that has actually read everything you uploaded.

But the bigger vision is more than a study tool. NotesOS is being built to apply real cognitive psychology and learning science to how it teaches you — the right methods, at the right time, to make things actually stick. And it follows you through your entire university journey, from year one to graduation, getting better the longer you use it and the more classmates join you on it.

The goal: you reach for NotesOS first — before every test, every exam, every time you need to understand something deeply.

---

## Who Uses It

University students — primarily undergrads — during exam prep and regular study. People who are already overwhelmed with content and want something that does the heavy lifting.

They're multitasking. Distracted. They come to this app when they genuinely want to understand something, not just skim it.

---

## How the App Is Structured

The organisation is: **Semesters → Courses → Topics**

- A **semester** groups your courses (e.g. "Semester 2 — 2026")
- A **course** is a subject (e.g. "Organic Chemistry")
- A **topic** is a specific chapter or concept (e.g. "Chapter 3: Alkenes")

Each **topic** is the main study unit — that's where everything lives.

## Every Screen in the App

### Home

The first screen after login. Shows a "Continue Studying" card with the last topic you were on, plus a quick-access grid of recently visited topics. If you're brand new, it prompts you to create your first course or join a classmate's semester.

---

### Sidebar (always visible)

The dark left panel is the main navigation. It shows all your courses and topics in a collapsible tree. The active topic is highlighted in amber. At the bottom there are buttons to create a new course or join a semester. This sidebar is always there — it's how you move around the whole app.

---

### Topic Page — the main screen

This is where users spend most of their time. When you open a topic, you see:

**Audio Lesson**
An audio version of the material, auto-generated. Has play/pause, a seek bar, speed controls (1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2×), and a rewind button. Users can dismiss it and bring it back.

**Key Points**
Bullet-point list of the main takeaways from the material. Clean, scannable.

**Consolidated Notes**
A full AI-written summary of everything you uploaded, formatted as proper study notes. This is the main reading surface — it gets the "notebook" treatment in the design (lined background, rough border).

**Concepts**
Term + definition pairs for all the core vocabulary in the material. Like a glossary.

**Sources**
All the files you uploaded — PDFs, images, typed notes. You can see thumbnails, click to view originals.

**AI Chat**
A floating "Ask AI" button in the corner opens a chat panel. You can ask the AI anything about the topic and it responds based on what you uploaded. On mobile it slides up from the bottom; on desktop it slides in from the side.

**Previous / Next Navigation**
At the bottom of the page, you can move to the next or previous topic in the course. Makes it easy to study sequentially.

**Adding Materials**
A "+ Add" button opens a modal where you can drag-and-drop files or paste text. Materials are processed in the background while you keep using the app.

---

### Focus Mode

A full-screen immersive reading view. All the sidebar and UI chrome disappears — just the content. On wide screens, there are small doodles in the left and right margins (like margin sketches in a real notebook). Press F to enter/exit. This is one of the most delightful parts of the app.

---

### Quiz Page

You can take AI-generated quizzes on any topic directly from the topic page, or generate a custom test from the "Generate Test" page. Quiz questions are multiple choice or short answer. You can type your answers or record your voice — the AI grades both. After submitting, you get a score and detailed feedback on each question.

---

### Generate Test

A page where you choose: which course, which topics (multi-select), how many questions, what type (multiple choice, short answer, or mixed), and difficulty level. Then generate. Past tests are saved so you can retake them.

---

### Progress Page

A stats dashboard. Shows your study streak, total study time, average quiz score, and topics studied. Then breaks it down per course — each topic has a mastery percentage and a status badge (Not started / Needs work / Good). There's also a list of recent quiz attempts.

---

### Settings

- **Profile** — edit your name and password
- **AI Personality** — choose the AI's tone (Encouraging, Direct, Humorous), explanation style (Concise, Detailed, Example-heavy), and emoji usage. This changes how the AI tutor talks to you across the whole app.
- **Semesters** — create, rename, set a current semester, copy an invite code to share with classmates
- **Notifications** — see app notifications, mark as read
- **Account** — sign out, delete account
