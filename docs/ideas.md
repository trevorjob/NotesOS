## Study rhythm

- **NotesOS-native pomodoro — rest as a spacing interval.** Not a generic timer: the block is
  *read/dump → rest (music / true rest / Listen) → open the next block by recapping the last one*.
  Rest-then-recap is distributed practice — the break IS the forgetting interval that makes the
  next retrieval count. "Rest is part of the method, not a pause from it." Owner's own pattern:
  30-min blocks × 3–4, chill music between. ⚡ warm close already exists as the block-end beat.
- ⚡ **Adaptive block length.** The attempt log has timestamps + grades — detect where recall
  quality degrades within a session ("your accuracy drops after ~34 min") and tune block length
  per user. A pomodoro that learned its interval from your data.
- **Pre-sleep five.** A tiny nightly review; reviewing before sleep measurably consolidates.
  Gentle, honest, on-brand nudge framing.

## Collab / communal

- ⚡ **Peer-gap teach matching.** `ConceptState` across users on shared concepts → the system
  knows you're shaky where a classmate is solid, and vice versa. Match them: you teach yours,
  they teach theirs. Teach-mode with a human stake; complementary, never competitive;
  recognition fires naturally.
- ⚡ **Group brain dump.** Everyone dumps on the same topic; merge coverage maps. Co-op output
  ("together you covered 92% — you carried X, Chidi carried Y") **plus the sleeper: collective
  failure on a concept is a NOTE-QUALITY signal** — if nobody surfaces concept 7, flag that note
  section for re-synthesis / bounty a better explanation. The class's forgetting debugs the
  material.
- **Study rooms / body-doubling.** Presence + WebSocket exist. Room = same topic, everyone doing
  their own retrieval, shared block timer (pomodoro × collab merge), shared warm close.
- **Explanation bounties.** "3 classmates are stuck on X — explain it?" A good teach-mode answer
  becomes an attributed note contribution (contribution path already a locked concept).

## Exam-aware

- **Exam date reshapes the engine.** FSRS retention dial + a deadline → honestly compressed
  intervals (cram-aware, not cram-denying). Next-action gets urgency: "14 days out — at your pace
  you'll cover the syllabus 1.2×."
- **Post-exam collective dump → emergent past-questions bank.** Dump what was tested right after
  the exam; merge across classmates → a crowd-reconstructed past paper attached to the course,
  **inherited by next year's cohort** (emergent-set continuity). Past questions are currency in
  Nigerian unis — this manufactures them communally. Possibly the strongest growth loop available.

## Knowledge / engine

- ⚡ **Confusion pairs.** Detect when a wrong answer to concept A is actually concept B ("you keep
  swapping mitosis/meiosis") → targeted discrimination drills. The unused `Concept.embedding`
  column is the natural substrate.
- **Prerequisite root-cause.** Edges between concepts: "you're failing X because prerequisite Y is
  weak." Debugging for knowledge.
- **Semester time capsule / "Wrapped".** Seal a dump at semester end, reopen next year; shareable
  year-in-review ("you revived 214 fading concepts") — Wrapped-style growth marketing with zero
  leaderboard toxicity.
- **Study transcript.** The append-only log is hard-to-fake evidence of mastery over time —
  exportable proof-of-knowledge (tutoring gigs, scholarships). Long shot; substrate-unique.

## Far edge

- **WhatsApp retrieval.** The decay nudge arrives on WhatsApp (where OTP already lives) *with the
  question inline* — reply to answer. Retrieval without opening the app, on the platform students
  actually live in.
